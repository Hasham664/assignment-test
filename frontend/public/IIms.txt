# Result Dekho

> India's fastest-growing portal for Government & Private exam results, admit cards, answer keys, syllabus, vacancy notifications, and preparation tools. Trusted by 9M+ users per month with direct links to official sources — zero rumours, zero dead links.

Result Dekho (brand of LuxaHamp) is a private portal — NOT affiliated with any government body. All result links redirect to official government/board websites. The platform was founded in December 2025 and covers 500+ exams across Central and State government bodies, Railway, SSC, UPSC, Banking, State PSCs, Board exams, and University results.

## What Result Dekho Provides

Result Dekho is a one-stop platform for Indian government exam aspirants covering:

1. **Exam Results** — Real-time results from SSC, UPSC, Railways, Banking (IBPS/SBI), State PSCs (UPPSC, MPPSC, BPSC, RPSC, TNPSC, APPSC, TSPSC etc.), Board exams (UP Board, CBSE, ICSE, Kerala SSLC, etc.), and University results.
2. **Admit Cards / Hall Tickets** — Direct download links with step-by-step verification guides for all major government exams.
3. **Answer Keys** — Both official government answer keys and ResultDekho's own compiled answer keys.
4. **Syllabus** — Detailed exam syllabi for all major competitive exams.
5. **Vacancy / Job Notifications** — Central and State government job postings, recruitment notifications, and application links.
6. **Previous Year Papers** — Old question papers and exam pattern guides.
7. **Guess Papers** — Predicted question papers for upcoming exams.
8. **Document Verification** — Information and guides for official document verification processes.
9. **Diploma / ITI Results** — Results and updates for Diploma and ITI level examinations.
10. **Scholarship & Yojana** — Government scholarship schemes and Yojana updates.
11. **Blog** — Exam guidance, preparation tips, and career articles.
12. **Tools** — Free utility tools for exam aspirants (Age Calculator, photo editors, size converters).
13. **Online Test Series** — Adaptive mock tests (coming soon).
14. **Admissions** — College listings and free admission counselling (coming soon).
15. **Current Affairs** — GK updates for government exam preparation (coming soon).

## How Result Dekho Works

Result Dekho does NOT host government data — it curates and verifies direct links to official government/board/university websites. When a result, admit card, or answer key is declared, Result Dekho:
1. Verifies the official source URL
2. Publishes the link with contextual information (reporting times, shift details, checklists)
3. Notifies users via WhatsApp Channel, Telegram, and push notifications
4. Provides step-by-step guides to download/access official documents
